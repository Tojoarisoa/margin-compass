# VER-14 follow-up: Harness and ICONIQ primary sources (2026-11-12)

PLAN.md §20.2 cited the Harness and ICONIQ figures through secondary summaries (VER-14, 2026-11-02). This pass
located the publishers' own pages and checked each figure against them.

**Method and limit:** web search on 2026-11-12. The publishers' hosts (`harness.io`, `iconiq.com`) do not resolve
from the cloud session, so the pages were read through the search index, not fetched, and the full PDFs were not
opened. Treat the figures as checked against the primary page summaries, not the report tables.

| Claim in §20.2 | Primary source | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 52% have no clear owner for AI costs | Harness, *State of AI in FinOps 2026* (harness.io/state-of-ai-in-finops-2026; press release "Enterprise AI spend has outgrown the systems built to track it"), survey of 700 engineering and FinOps leaders, released 2026-07-29 | Confirmed: more than half (52%) have no clear, dedicated AI cost owner. Same page: 72% had a surprise AI bill in the past year. |
| AI gross margins around 52% | ICONIQ, *2026 State of AI: Bi-Annual Snapshot* (January 2026, iconiq.com/growth/insights/state-of-ai-bi-annual-snapshot) | Confirmed with a wording fix: average **AI product** gross margin 52% (2026), up from 45% (2025) and 41% (2024), across software companies building AI products, not "AI-native" companies only. The later *Builder's Economy* report (Q2 2026, ~305 executives) is reported at 53%. |
| 75-85% for traditional SaaS | Not in ICONIQ's pages | Kept as a general SaaS benchmark, no longer attributed to ICONIQ. |
| Inference ≈ 20% of total spend pre-launch, ≈ 23% when scaling | ICONIQ snapshot (cost breakdown) | Confirmed as share of **total spend** (talent 32% -> 26%, cloud ~17%), all surveyed companies. |
| Consumption-based pricing 19% -> 35%; outcome-based 2% -> 18% | ICONIQ snapshot (January 2026) | Confirmed for the January 2026 snapshot. The Q2 2026 *Builder's Economy* report has 42% and 23% (subscription 57%). |

No KPI, target or simulator parameter depends on these figures; §1 already says "around the 50% range".
