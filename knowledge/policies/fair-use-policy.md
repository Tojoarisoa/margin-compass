# BoonCore AI Fair-Use Policy v1

> Synthetic document for the Margin Compass portfolio project. BoonCore Inc. is fictional.

## 1. Purpose and scope
<!-- clause: scope -->

This policy sets the fair-use limits for the BoonCore AI add-on. It applies to every BoonCore tenant
on the Starter, Growth, and Enterprise plans unless the tenant's order form says otherwise. The AI
add-on covers Reply Copilot (F1), the Device Troubleshooting Agent (F2), Service Knowledge Search (F3),
and Case & Complaint Summaries (F4).

## 2. Included AI usage per plan
<!-- clause: usage_allowance -->

The AI add-on includes a monthly allowance of AI-assisted tickets. A ticket counts once, however many
AI features it uses.

| Plan | Included AI-assisted tickets per month *(assumption)* |
| --- | --- |
| Starter | 250 |
| Growth | 1,100 |
| Enterprise | 6,600 |

Unused allowance does not roll over to the next month.

## 3. Safety Screening is always included
<!-- clause: safety_screening -->

Safety Screening (F0) runs on every ticket and never counts against the allowance. BoonCore will not
disable, throttle, or downgrade Safety Screening to manage cost, and no fair-use measure in this
policy applies to it.

## 4. Usage above the allowance
<!-- clause: overage -->

If a tenant uses more than 150% of its monthly allowance for two consecutive months, BoonCore opens a
usage review with the tenant's account team. This policy version does not charge overage fees. Any
overage or per-resolution pricing needs a signed amendment to the tenant's order form.

## 5. Legacy unlimited AI clauses
<!-- clause: unlimited_ai -->

Some older order forms contain an "unlimited AI" clause. Where an order form grants unlimited AI
usage, the monthly allowance in section 2 does not apply for the current term. The clause does not
renew automatically: at renewal, the account team must either reprice the AI add-on or move the
tenant to the standard allowance. Fair-use limits on automated or abusive traffic (section 6) still
apply to unlimited tenants.

## 6. Prohibited and abusive use
<!-- clause: prohibited_use -->

The AI add-on must not be used for automated bulk traffic that is not linked to a real support
ticket, for load testing, or to send medical images or DICOM data. BoonCore may rate-limit traffic
that breaks this section after notifying the tenant's admin contact.

## 7. Model routing
<!-- clause: model_routing -->

BoonCore may change which AI model serves a feature to manage quality and cost, provided the tenant's
approved-model list and data-residency terms are respected. Routing changes never reduce Safety
Screening recall below BoonCore's 99% floor.

## 8. Changes to this policy
<!-- clause: policy_changes -->

BoonCore gives tenants 60 days' written notice before a change to this policy takes effect. A change
never shortens a tenant's current term or overrides a signed order form.
